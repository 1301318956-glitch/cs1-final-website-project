Why I Like Them

Sasuke Uchiha and Kisame Hoshigaki are powerful shinobi from Naruto, known for their distinct abilities and association with Itachi Uchiha. Sasuke, an Uchiha prodigy, wields the Sharingan and lightning jutsu, while Kisame, a Seven Swordsman of the Mist, uses the chakra-absorbing sword Samehada, immense water release, and immense chakra.

I Like Them Because There Style's And Also Because Their Abilitys And Skills.
